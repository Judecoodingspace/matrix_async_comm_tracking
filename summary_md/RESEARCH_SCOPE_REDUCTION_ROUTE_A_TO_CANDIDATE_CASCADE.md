# RESEARCH SCOPE REDUCTION: ROUTE A TO CANDIDATE CASCADE

Status: `WORKSTREAM_SPLIT_FROZEN`

## A. Why scope reduction

The current Work 1 mechanism is a narrow, already observed control-flow effect:
ID-state timing changes the current-frame old-unmatched membership consumed by
High-score Supplement. The frozen E023 source recomputes this membership after
three ID-state mutation/delivery stages and immediately passes it to
`not_matched_supplement()`. At delay 5, formal evidence shows that the changed
membership creates successful Supplement write-ins that partially compensate
for the direct harm of delayed ID state.

Route A addresses a materially larger question. Its existing plans introduce
immutable observation identity, receiver lineage and tombstones, same-capture
feature snapshots, a candidate-independent spatial provider, offline GT
firewalls, development selection, held-out readiness, a later temporal bridge,
arrival-time candidate handling, hypothesis newness and eventual future-state
use. Even Stage A alone has six implementation milestones and independent
availability, exclusion, anti-triviality and reproducibility gates. That work
is scientifically coherent, but it is not necessary to test whether the
already existing MIA Supplement eligibility transition can be stabilized.

Therefore Work 1 must not inherit Route A's general late-observation reuse
claim or its spatial/temporal representation burden.

## B. Workstream split

### Work 1 — current paper

Question: does asynchrony alter association candidate membership and
Supplement eligibility in a way that causes both destructive cascade and
unexpected compensation, and can a minimal deployment-time intervention
stabilize that existing opportunity?

Required story:

```text
phenomenon
-> candidate-set mechanism
-> causal validation
-> minimal intervention
-> formal evaluation
```

Work 1 is restricted to the current MIA same-frame state transition:

```text
ID-state delivery/visibility
-> recomputed old-unmatched membership
-> High-score Supplement consumption
-> tracker consequence
```

It does not authorize general candidate generation or delayed-observation
reprojection.

### Work 2 — Route A future work

Route A remains the general causal late-observation reuse framework:

```text
late observation
-> spatial reinterpretation
-> temporal bridge
-> arrival-time current candidate
-> new association opportunity
-> identity evidence
-> future-state use
```

Current status:

```text
PARKED_AFTER_PLANNING
```

`PARKED_AFTER_PLANNING` means neither failed nor abandoned. It means Route A is
not a prerequisite, implementation target or evaluation dependency of Work 1.

  new-association opportunity, identity recovery or tracking gain.

No Route A plan, decision or negative result is deleted or reinterpreted as a
Work 1 result. Route A development and held-out remain unexecuted.

ROUTE_A_NOT_EXECUTED_IN_THIS_TASK

## D. What is no longer blocking Work 1

The following are not Work 1 blockers:

- Spatial Bridge or candidate-independent feasible-region readiness;
- Temporal Bridge;
- Homography, Fundamental Matrix, BEV, optical/learned flow, camera pose or
  calibration;
- arbitrary late-observation spatial reprojection;
- observation-to-current-live-track resolution for general delayed reuse;
- general new-association-opportunity measurement;
- Route A Stage-A development or held-out execution.

Work 1 may use only evidence already available at the current MIA same-frame
association boundary. If a later source audit proves that a proposed Work 1
intervention cannot operate without one of the items above, that is a new
Research Decision and the proposal must stop rather than silently expand.
